THE INSTITUTION

Bangladesh Agricultural University — Legacy, Research & Global Standing

Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU) has been one of the most influential institutions in the development of agricultural education, research and professional capacity in Bangladesh.

Its academic environment connects agriculture with food security, agricultural economics, technology, rural development, agribusiness, fisheries, livestock and sustainable resource management.

In the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026, BAU is placed in the 1001–1200 world ranking band and 601–800 in Life Sciences. For me, BAU's importance goes beyond ranking: it is the institution where agriculture became a complete academic, social and professional perspective.

Research Culture

Applied research addressing food, livelihoods, markets, climate, rural development and agricultural transformation.

National Impact

Agricultural knowledge connected directly with farmers, communities, policy, markets and national food systems.

Academic Depth

A multidisciplinary agricultural environment that connects science, economics, society and innovation.

THE DEGREE

Agricultural Economics & Rural Sociology — What I Studied

Agricultural Economics taught me to see agriculture as an economic system, while Rural Sociology taught me to understand the people and communities behind that system.

Together, the discipline developed a broader understanding of how production, markets, rural livelihoods, finance, policy, society and development interact.

Agricultural Economics

Production economics, resource allocation, costs, returns and economic decision-making in agriculture.

Agricultural Markets

Supply, demand, pricing, market structure, value chains and agricultural marketing.

Statistics & Analysis

Data interpretation, quantitative analysis and evidence-based decision-making.

Agricultural Finance

Understanding finance, investment, credit and economic decision-making in agricultural systems.

Rural Sociology

Rural institutions, communities, social relationships, livelihoods and development processes.

Agribusiness

Connecting agriculture with business, markets, entrepreneurship and commercial opportunities.

MY FIRST HOME AWAY FROM HOME

Shahid Nazmul Ahsan Hall — The Life Classroom

Leaving my family for university, Shahid Nazmul Ahsan Hall became my first real home away from home.

Hall life became one of the most important learning environments of my university journey. Living with people from different backgrounds taught me unity, adaptability, respect, communication, cooperation and how to become part of a community.

What Hall Life Taught Me

From speaking in front of others to coordinating dining-related activities, helping organize tours, supporting hall programmes, participating in competitions and managing collective responsibilities, I gradually developed practical leadership and people-management skills.

At the same time, university life was never only responsibility. There were classes, examinations, assignments, friendships and countless memorable addas. Learning how to balance all of these taught me one of my most valuable capabilities: time management.
