KEY ACCOUNT MANAGER

Pinnacle Foods Sdn Bhd (PRAN-RFL Group Affiliate)

1. THE FOUNDATION: LEARNING AT ECO EXPO

My professional journey began during the COVID-19 pandemic with Eco Expo, a prominent event management firm known for organizing international trade fairs. Serving as a Project Manager and Communication Consultant, I navigated the challenges of online business operations. This high-pressure environment taught me to handle international buyers, make instant decisions, and master time management—skills that became the cornerstone of my career.

2. THE MALAYSIA STRUGGLE & MEETING MR. PARVEZ HIRA

Moving to Malaysia in 2024 to pursue my Master's was a journey of resilience. Balancing family, high living costs, and a 22km university commute, I worked exhausting 10-hour shifts at a Chinese restaurant just to survive. My turning point came when I approached Pinnacle Foods Sdn Bhd. Meeting Mr. Parvez Hira (Head of Sales, Modern Trade) right on the road as he left for a market visit was divine intervention. He tested my determination by questioning if I could handle the rigorous field sales life—and I proved that I could.

3. ASCENSION: FROM SALESMAN TO KEY ACCOUNT MANAGER

After my training, I was assigned key routes including Bukit Bintang, Kota Damansara, and Genting Highlands. Applying the professional dedication learned at Eco Expo, I shattered historical sales records and remained the company's Best Salesman for 7–8 consecutive months.

Promoted to Regional Manager, I continued to break the benchmarks set by my predecessors. Today, as a Key Account Manager, I lead with the same hunger and dedication that saw me through my initial days in Malaysia.

4. MISSION: PINNACLE FOODS #1

My goal is simple: through unwavering dedication, I strive to make Pinnacle Foods the #1 company in Malaysia. With the strength, wisdom, and perseverance granted by the Almighty, I remain committed to serving with excellence. I humbly ask for your prayers as I continue this journey.
