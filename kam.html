Professional Journey in Sales & Key Account Management

My career in Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) sales management is rooted in a passion for driving market expansion, building strategic commercial relationships, and executing high-impact retail initiatives. Starting with international sales representation at Eco Expo, I gained invaluable global exposure in connecting businesses and expanding market footprints. Building upon that foundation, my journey evolved into spearheading modern trade operations as a Key Account Manager and Regional Manager for Pinnacle Foods Sdn Bhd, an esteemed affiliate of the renowned PRAN-RFL Group in Malaysia.

Pinnacle Foods & PRAN-RFL Group: Modern Trade & Retail Merchandising

Managing modern trade accounts across major hypermarket chains in Malaysia—such as NSK Grocer, NSK Trade City, and Giant Hypermarket—requires rigorous planning, dynamic execution, and proactive leadership. At Pinnacle Foods, I oversee vital product lines including PRAN Basil Seed drinks, fruit juices, soy milk, potato crackers, and instant noodles. My role encompasses orchestrating promotional combo offers, designing retail display planograms, and formulating robust trade marketing strategies that consistently elevate brand visibility and boost sales velocity.

Leadership, Strategy & Core Strengths

Succeeding in competitive FMCG markets demands a unique blend of strategic foresight and rapid problem-solving capabilities.
