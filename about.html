ABOUT ME
The Journey of MEHEDI ALIM AYON
Agriculturist, Communicationist and Account Management Professional whose journey has been shaped by discipline, leadership, continuous learning, family responsibility and a strong ambition to create meaningful professional impact.

Introduction & Early Life

Who I Am: I am Mehedi Alim Ayon, born on 10 May 1999 in Rangpur, Bangladesh, to my beloved father Abu Hena Mostofa and mother Anjuman Ara Begum.

My father is a retired Bangladesh Army officer, and growing up as the son of a military man had a strong influence on my discipline, courage, responsibility and personality.

From childhood, I was energetic, adventurous, determined and independent by nature. My parents always encouraged me to learn, remain disciplined and develop myself through education and experience.

One of the most important lessons I received from my father was the value of self-dependence, discipline and responsibility. Those principles have remained an important part of my life.

Education & Academic Journey

Because of my father's military career, I experienced different environments during my early education. This helped me develop the ability to adapt quickly to new places, people and situations.

  • Primary Education: I completed my early schooling in Bangladesh and developed a strong foundation in academic learning and discipline.
  • PSC: I achieved Golden GPA 5 in my Primary School Certificate.
  • JSC: I completed my JSC from Rangpur Cantonment Public School & College with GPA 5.
  • SSC: I completed my SSC from Rangpur Cantonment Public School & College with Golden GPA 5.
  • HSC: I completed my Higher Secondary education with a strong academic foundation and continued my journey toward agricultural science.
  • BSc: I completed my Bachelor of Science from Bangladesh Agricultural University.
  • Master's Degree: I completed my Master's degree from Kuala Lumpur University of Science & Technology.

By the grace of Almighty Allah, I remained consistent throughout my academic life and always tried to achieve the highest possible result while developing myself beyond the classroom.

BNCC, Leadership & Confidence

One of the most influential chapters of my school life was my involvement with the Bangladesh National Cadet Corps (BNCC).

I joined BNCC during my school years, and the experience changed many aspects of my personality. It taught me discipline, leadership, teamwork, public speaking, communication, confidence and decision-making.

I participated in various quiz competitions, cultural programs, debates, BNCC camps, drills and other activities. Through my dedication and participation, I received several medals, awards and recognitions.

At a young age, I was given leadership responsibilities within my division. I also received recognition for my drill performance and leadership abilities.

One of the most memorable achievements of my BNCC journey was being selected as one of only a few cadets from Bangladesh for a Cadet Ambassador Tour to Karnataka, India.

These experiences became the foundation of my communication skills, leadership ability, convincing power, team management and professional confidence.

Through university life, we came to know each other and eventually became life partners. Today, we are living together in Malaysia and continuing our academic and professional journeys.

Since childhood, I have always wanted to become self-dependent. My parents have always supported me, but I personally wanted to build my own path and learn how to stand on my own feet.

I believe in hard work, discipline, honesty, continuous learning, respect for people and taking responsibility for every opportunity Allah gives me.

I also believe that difficult situations are often the greatest teachers. Every struggle, challenge and uncertain period of life teaches us something that success alone cannot teach.
