Mehedi Alim Ayon
About Me | Personal Journey | Family & Life

Who I Am

I am Mehedi Alim Ayon. I was born on 10 May 1999 in Rangpur, Bangladesh, to my beloved father Abu Hena Mostofa and mother Anjuman Ara Begum. My father is a retired Bangladesh Army officer, and growing up as the son of a military man had a profound influence on my personality, discipline, courage and sense of responsibility.

From childhood, I was energetic, adventurous, determined and naturally curious. I was never afraid of taking responsibility or standing in front of people. My parents always encouraged me to learn, explore, remain disciplined and become independent in life.

One of the strongest principles I learned from my father was self-dependence. He taught me through his own life that a person should first learn to stand on his own feet, take responsibility for his decisions and work hard before expecting anything from others.

Childhood, Education & Academic Journey

Because of my father's military career, I spent different stages of my educational life in different places. This gave me the opportunity to experience different environments, cultures and communities from an early age.

  • Primary Education: My early schooling took place in different locations, including Balya and Cumilla, before continuing at Adamjee Cantonment School & College.
  • PSC: I achieved a Golden GPA 5 in my Primary School Certificate examination.
  • JSC: I completed my Junior School Certificate from Rangpur Cantonment Public School & College with GPA 5.
  • SSC: I completed my Secondary School Certificate from Rangpur Cantonment Public School & College with Golden GPA 5.

Throughout my academic life, by the grace of Almighty Allah, I remained consistent in my studies and always tried to achieve the highest possible result.

BNCC, Leadership & The Beginning of My Confidence

One of the most important chapters of my early life was my involvement with the Bangladesh National Cadet Corps (BNCC). I joined BNCC during my school years, and this experience transformed many aspects of my personality.

BNCC taught me discipline, leadership, courage, public speaking, teamwork, communication, management and the ability to make decisions under pressure. It was one of the most important platforms that helped me become confident in front of people.

I participated in numerous BNCC camps, quiz competitions, debates, cultural programs, drills and other activities. Through dedication and hard work, I received various awards, medals and recognitions.

At a remarkably young age, I was entrusted with leadership responsibilities within my division. I also had the honour of receiving recognition for my drill performance and leadership abilities.

One of the memorable achievements of my BNCC journey was being selected as one of only a few cadets from Bangladesh for an international cadet ambassador tour to Karnataka, India. The experience broadened my understanding of leadership, teamwork, culture and international exposure.

I participated in multiple camps and frequently served in leadership responsibilities. These experiences gave me a strong foundation in leadership, communication, confidence, convincing ability and people management.

My Personality, Interests & Passions

I have always considered myself a person who enjoys learning, exploring and taking responsibility. I naturally like challenges and I enjoy situations where I have to think quickly and find practical solutions.

Personally, I love sports, especially football. I also have a strong passion for personal appearance, fashion and dressing well. I enjoy collecting shoes, perfumes, shirts, T-shirts, traditional Panjabi and other clothing items.

Another major fascination of mine is cars. I have always been interested in automobiles and I enjoy learning about different types of vehicles.

For me, looking organized is not simply about appearance. It is connected with discipline, confidence and self-respect.

University Life & The Beginning of My Family

After completing my school and college education, I joined Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU). My university life became one of the most important chapters of my personal and professional development.

It was also during my university life that an important chapter of my personal life began. My wife, Nusrat Nasrin, was my immediate junior at Bangladesh Agricultural University and was associated with the Fisheries Faculty and Sultanah Ziahan Hall.

Through university life, we came to know each other and eventually became life partners. Today, we are together in Malaysia, continuing our education, professional careers and family journey.

My Wife & Her Journey

My wife, Nusrat Nasrin, is one of the most important sources of support in my life.

She completed her education at Bangladesh Agricultural University and later pursued an MBA in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. She is currently enrolled in a research program in Aquaculture at a university in Malaysia.

Alongside her academic journey, she is also professionally active and currently works as the Head of Marketing at a travel agency.

Her dedication to research, education and professional development continues to inspire me, and we support each other in our individual journeys.

My Family & My Younger Brother

I come from a family where my parents have always been my strongest source of support. My father and mother currently live in Bangladesh, while my wife, my younger brother and I are currently based in Malaysia.

My younger brother, Wasif Ishaq Orpon, is also currently in Malaysia. He is pursuing his studies at Asia Metropolitan University while also working alongside his education.

We are two brothers, and although we are living away from our parents, their love, guidance and blessings remain an essential part of our lives.

We are also fortunate to have our cousins around us in Malaysia, which gives us a strong family environment while living abroad.

My Values & Life Philosophy

I believe that success is not simply about money, position or recognition. For me, true success means becoming capable enough to take responsibility for myself and my family.

From childhood, I have wanted to become self-dependent. My parents always had the ability to support me, but I personally wanted to build my own path and learn how to stand on my own feet.

I believe in hard work, discipline, honesty, continuous learning, respect for people and taking responsibility for every opportunity Allah gives me.

I also believe that every difficult situation teaches something. The struggles, failures, uncertainty and challenges of life are not necessarily obstacles; sometimes they are the training ground for the person we are meant to become.

The People Behind My Journey

Whatever I have achieved so far, I never consider it a journey that I completed alone.

My parents, my wife, my younger brother and my entire family have always been my strongest support system. They have encouraged me, trusted me and never stopped me from pursuing my dreams.

Their prayers, sacrifices and support give me the strength to keep moving forward even when the road becomes difficult.

I pray that Almighty Allah grants my parents, my wife, my brother and every member of my family a long, healthy and blessed life.

“The day I become capable of fulfilling my responsibilities toward my parents, earning their respect and making my family proud — that will be the day I will truly consider myself successful.”

My Journey Continues

Today, I am living in Malaysia with my family and continuing my professional and personal journey. I am grateful for every chapter of my life — the easy moments, the difficult moments, the lessons, the people I met and every opportunity Allah has given me.

I know that my journey is far from complete. There are still many things I want to learn, many goals I want to achieve and many responsibilities I want to fulfil.

With the blessings of my parents, the support of my family, the lessons of my past and the mercy of Almighty Allah, I will continue moving forward with courage, humility and determination.

Please keep me and my family in your prayers.
