COMMUNICATION & CORPORATE JOURNEY

EARLY BEGINNINGS AT ECO EXPO (2022)

My professional communication journey began in 2022 with Eco Expo, a renowned international exhibition and fair organizing company (including Bangladesh-China Friendship Exhibition Center fairs). During the challenging times of COVID-19, this role provided me with an exceptional platform to build essential professional skills while working remotely. As an English Consultant and overall Project Manager, I handled overseas buyers, managed client communications via Zoom, made instant high-pressure decisions, and fostered cross-border connections. This foundational period shaped my resilience, hard work, and sharp communication acumen before I transitioned back to complete my graduation from Bangladesh Agricultural University and moved to Malaysia.

JOINING PRAN PINNACLE FOODS (MALAYSIA)

By the grace of Almighty Allah, on December 13, 2024, I officially stepped into the Malaysian corporate arena with Pran Pinnacle Foods Sdn Bhd (an affiliate of PRAN-RFL Group), proudly carrying the flag of Bangladesh to international markets. During my interview, Head of Sales (Modern Trade) Mr. Parvez Hira recognized my potential, confidence, and passion, giving me the breakthrough opportunity to start as a Sales Representative.

Through relentless hard work, strategic execution, and exceptional sales performance, I achieved top performer status and secured consecutive monthly incentives, proving that dedication can conquer any market.

RAPID PROMOTION: REGIONAL MANAGER TO KEY ACCOUNT MANAGER

Supported by our Managing Director, Head of Sales Mr. Parvez Hira, CEO, and our incredible team, I was promoted to Regional Manager (Sales) for the Kuala Lumpur Zone after just 7 months. Within 4 months of taking leadership, I broke previous records by achieving maximum sales targets, securing top performance, and successfully listing 17 new retail accounts.

Owing to my digital mindset, quick decision-making, and unique promotional strategies, I was further elevated to become the youngest Key Account Manager (KAM) in the company within just 1.5 years. Today, I proudly manage major modern trade units including Giant Hypermarket and NSK Grocer.
