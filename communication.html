COMMUNICATION & CORPORATE JOURNEY

The foundation of my career is built upon my academic roots in Agricultural Economics and Rural Sociology from Bangladesh Agricultural University. This background taught me how to analyze market dynamics, understand community needs, and establish effective communication.

BEGINNING AT PRAN PINNACLE FOODS (MALAYSIA)

While pursuing my Master's degree in Malaysia, I officially stepped into the corporate world in December 2024 with Pran Pinnacle Foods Sdn Bhd (an affiliate of PRAN-RFL Group). Head of Sales (Modern Trade) Mr. Parvez Hira selected me during the interview, giving me the opportunity to represent Bangladesh's pride across international markets. Starting as a Sales Representative in Bukit Bintang and prime zones of Kuala Lumpur, I secured top performance and incentives for 6-7 consecutive months.

RAPID GROWTH: REGIONAL MANAGER TO KEY ACCOUNT MANAGER

With the support of our Managing Director, Head of Sales Mr. Parvez Hira, and the entire team, I was promoted to Regional Manager (Sales) for Kuala Lumpur Zone within 7 months. Achieving top sales targets and 17 new account listings in a short span, I later advanced to become the youngest Key Account Manager (KAM) at Pinnacle Foods within 1.5 years.
