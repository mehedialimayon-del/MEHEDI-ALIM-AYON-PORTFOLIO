MY JOURNEY & ACADEMIC PURSUIT

FROM VISION TO EXECUTION IN MALAYSIA

THE DECISION FOR OVERSEAS EDUCATION

My aspiration for higher education abroad stems from a long-standing vision to pursue advanced academic degrees and ultimately earn a doctorate. Completing my Bachelor's degree from Bangladesh Agricultural University laid a strong foundation, after which I set my sights on international exposure. However, traditional Western options often involve massive financial burdens and unpredictable, lengthy visa procedures that can stretch for a year or more.

Refusing to rely on heavy parental funding, I decided to utilize the savings I accumulated during my undergraduate years. I wanted a smart, efficient path without wasting precious time on endless waiting periods. Despite common misconceptions and negative reviews surrounding Malaysia, I remained steadfast in my conviction: success depends on personal grit and hard work, regardless of geography. If I was willing to put in the effort, Malaysia offered the ideal launchpad.

MASTER'S IN COMMUNICATION AT KLUST

Recognizing communication as a vital cornerstone across global industries, I chose to pursue a Master's degree in Communication at the Kuala Lumpur University of Science and Technology (KLUST). Under the esteemed guidance and continuous support of my Dean, Dr. Nasik Nizam, I navigated the rigorous academic environment with absolute dedication.

Through strategic focus and sharp execution, I achieved departmental topper status with a flawless 4.0 CGPA (First Class First). This academic milestone not only sharpened my theoretical expertise in communication, cross-cultural buyer handling, and rapid crisis management, but also empowered me to seamlessly bridge the gap between academic theory and high-stakes corporate leadership in Malaysia.
